## How to read this
- `objdiff` + `ninja configure.py` + `wibo` dominates GameCube/Wii/Xbox 360 (dtk-template lineage); `splat` + `make` + `asm-differ`/`decomp-permuter` dominates N64/PS1/PS2/GBA; Windows PC projects split between `reccmp` (annotated source) and dtk/objdiff configurations; Switch uses clang with cmake.
- Progress medians are not comparable across platforms (GBA/N64/PS1 are small games and often 100%; PS2/Wii/Switch are large).
- To dig deeper: `python3 <gamedecomp-library>/scripts/query.py --platform "<name>" --min-progress 50 --sort progress`.
